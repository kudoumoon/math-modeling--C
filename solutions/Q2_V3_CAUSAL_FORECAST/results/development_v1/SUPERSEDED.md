# Superseded Evidence

This development experiment is preserved for audit only.

The B0 PV forecast for a plan day's final slot used the previous plan day's final-slot observation at 00:00, although that interval completes at 00:10 under time mapping A. The causal correction invalidates the prior P1 approval, four-arm selection, and all annual runs derived from it.

Do not use this directory for model selection or as a Q3/Q4 upstream. The replacement experiment is `results/development_v2`.
